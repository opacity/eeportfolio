01 / Who I Am

Built to a Higher Standard

I'm an electrical engineering student obsessed with optimization — pulling more performance, more reliability, and more capability out of every circuit, every line of firmware, every system. Hardware pushed to its full potential is how great things get built, and that's the standard I hold my work to.

Building on my interest in cybersecurity, I am shifting my focus toward engineering physical defense hardware, specifically targeting Counter-UAS for defense, I apply this cross-disciplinary background directly to low-latency embedded systems, real-time threat detection, and electronic countermeasures development.

My interest in nuclear technology is grounded in execution. To help accelerate development timelines and advocate for the industry's future, I built SMRDB.com, By turning complex data on small modular reactors into an open, accessible database, this project serves as a technical resource designed to push development boundaries and spread essential awareness to the cause.

Photolithography is on my radar too — the process that turns sand into chips used in everything. For now it's an interest rather than a pursuit: the tooling lives in billion-dollar cleanrooms, and that's not a door a student budget opens. But I follow the field closely, and when the opportunity shows up, I intend to be ready for it.

4+ years of work so far, from simple C++ systems to breadboard circuits. Every project below taught me something I kept.

I'm a perfectionist, an idealist, and an optimist — and I intend to change the world with engineering made in America.
